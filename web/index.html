CNC Machining Calculators

Free CNC machining calculators for spindle speed, feed rate, feed per tooth, cutting speed, material removal rate (MRR), and drilling parameters. These tools are designed to support CNC machining, process development, and manufacturing engineering.

Spindle Speed

Formula n = (1000 × Vc) / (π × D)

n = Spindle Speed (RPM)

Vc = Cutting Speed (m/min)

D = Diameter (mm)
